\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
% =============================================================

The pipeline introduced here makes gradient-based waveform design
tractable on the same compute budget that gradient-free pipelines pay
for a single forward pass, with sub-millivolt per-site residuals
against NEURON on a real BBP cortical morphology under extracellular
stimulation. Direct optimisation of $w(t)$ against
spike-elicitation and selectivity targets is the natural next step
(\cref{sec:limitations}).
